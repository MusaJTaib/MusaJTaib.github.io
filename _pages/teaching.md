During my M.Sc., I served as a Graduate Teaching Assistant in the Department of Electrical and Software Engineering at the Schulich School of Engineering, University of Calgary, for three terms:

- Winter 2022
- Fall 2022
- Winter 2023

My responsibilities included leading labs on topics ranging from machine learning to network protocols, mentoring student project teams, and administering technical exams.

Teaching is a big part of the academic career I am working towards, and I look forward to more teaching opportunities during my Ph.D.
